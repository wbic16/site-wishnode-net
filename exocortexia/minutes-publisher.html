Minutes Publisher

Open Meetings Act compliance publishing · Neb. Rev. Stat. §§ 84-1408–84-1414

What it does

Public bodies — village boards, county boards, school boards, natural resources districts, city councils, fire districts — must post agendas before meetings and minutes after them, on statutory deadlines. Minutes Publisher tracks twelve synthetic Nebraska-flavoured public bodies, six meetings each, and checks every meeting against the Act's timing rules automatically.

  • Timeliness is computed, not remembered. Each agenda and minutes posting is checked against the statute's deadlines; a late posting shows as late.
  • Proof of posting. Every posting is recorded in a hash-chained audit log, so "we posted it on time" is a verifiable chain, not a recollection.
  • A publishable artifact. A single self-contained HTML snapshot — the page a body can actually post — closes with the audit entries (sequence, action, hash prefix) and the result of verifying the chain.

Statute mapping

Neb. Rev. Stat. § 84-1413 and the Open Meetings Act (§§ 84-1408–84-1414): the agenda must be available at least 24 hours before the meeting, minutes available within 10 working days, and minutes retained at least 6 months for the bodies the statute names. The full mapping, with template RFP language, is in the repository: docs/minutes-publisher-statute.md.
