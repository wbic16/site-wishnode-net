Contract & Grant Ledger

A feeder and reconciler for the Nebraska State Contracts Database · Neb. Rev. Stat. § 84-602.04

What it does

State contracts must be uploaded to the public State Contracts Database. In practice, agency records and the public database drift apart: uploads are missed, and amendments change authorized amounts without the public record showing the overlay. The Ledger keeps the agency-side record and reconciles it against the public side.

  • Amendment overlay. A contract's effective authorized amount is its base authorization plus every amendment delta. Base records are never modified; amendments are separate records that point at the base.
  • Expiry alerts. Every contract is bucketed as EXPIRED, or expiring within 30, 60, or 90 days, as of today.
  • Missing-upload flag. Contracts present in agency records but absent from the public database are badged MISSING FROM PUBLIC DATABASE — that badge is the reconciler's work queue: the uploads to chase.

The demo ledger carries 60 synthetic contracts, large enough for the drift patterns — missed uploads, unoverlaid amendments, quiet expiries — to be visible at a glance.

Statute mapping

Neb. Rev. Stat. § 84-602.04 (enacted by LB 429) requires state contracts to be published in the State Contracts Database, the public search at statecontracts.nebraska.gov. The full mapping, with template RFP language, is in the repository: docs/contract-ledger-statute.md.
